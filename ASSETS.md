# Visual assets
Built-in ImageGen was used for two project assets. The app displays rectangular viewports into the single atlas without editing the raster cells. Optional user JPGs still override these cells.

## images/wellness-atlas.png
Prompt: Create ONE cohesive image asset: a precisely aligned 4-column by 4-row PHOTO SPRITE ATLAS for a premium Thai mobile health tracker. Canvas square 2048x2048, sixteen equal square cells, zero gaps, zero borders, absolutely no text/labels/letters/watermarks/UI. Each cell self-contained, important subjects fully centered within middle 80%, same pale light-blue or warm white studio background, soft natural light, realistic photographic detail. Exact row-major subjects: row1: (1) plate of two boiled egg halves with cucumber and tomato; (2) grilled chicken breast slices with green salad; (3) steamed white fish fillet with broccoli and carrots; (4) bowl plain Greek yogurt. Row2: (1) white tofu and clear vegetable soup; (2) tuna in water salad; (3) cooked shrimp with mushrooms in clear soup; (4) plate steamed mixed vegetables. Row3: (1) adult athletic woman black sportswear doing bodyweight squat side three-quarter view, all body visible; (2) same woman standard push-up, straight torso, side view, all body visible; (3) same woman doing small sit-up with bent knees and hands across chest, side view; (4) same woman forearm plank side view straight torso. Row4: (1) same woman split stance lunge, side view; (2) same woman glute bridge shoulders on floor knees bent hips raised, side view; (3) same woman walking in light minimalist studio; (4) same woman seated gentle hamstring stretch. Photos only. No typography. This is one website sprite-sheet asset, not a poster or app screenshot.

## images/sunrise.png
Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: wide background photograph for the header of a premium mobile health tracker. Calm sunrise over a quiet green park and a distant lake with soft hills. Very pale powder-blue sky occupying the upper two thirds, a hint of soft warm gold sunrise at the far left horizon, blurred grass and distant trees only in the bottom third. Dreamy, subtle and uplifting, realistic photography with low contrast and generous bright negative space for dark navy interface text. Landscape 3:2 composition. No people, no buildings, no writing, no logos or UI.
